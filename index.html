Frequently Answered Doubts (LIC Trust)

Q: Kya LIC me paisa doobne ka koi risk hai?

Ans: Bilkul nahi! LIC Government of India ki statutory corporation hai jise 100% Sovereign Guarantee milti hai. Iska matlab hai ki aapka ek-ek paisa sarkar ki guarantee ke sath secure hai.

Q: Partner ko commission ya payout kaise milta hai?

Ans: Jaise hi aapka refer kiya hua customer genuine policy process complete kar leta hai, aapka payout direct aapke UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe) par ₹500 tak transfer kar diya jata hai.

Q: Lead submit karne ke baad kitna time lagta hai payout aane mein?

Ans: Policy verify aur complete hone ke baad, typically kuch working days ke andar payout process ho jata hai. Aap apne dashboard mein har lead ka live status (Under Process / Policy Completed / No Response / Rejected) dekh sakte ho.

Q: Agar mera lead "No Response" ya "Rejected" show ho raha hai toh?

Ans: Iska matlab customer contact nahi ho paya ya policy process aage nahi badha. Aap chaho toh naya updated number ke sath dobara same lead resubmit kar sakte ho, ya support se WhatsApp par baat kar sakte ho.

Q: Kya main ek se zyada lead submit kar sakta hoon?

Ans: Haan, jitne genuine leads chaho utne submit kar sakte ho — har converted lead par alag se payout milta hai. Sirf duplicate ya fake entries avoid karo.

Q: Website use karne ke liye koi charge lagta hai?

Ans: Nahi, SathiCare partner banna aur leads refer karna bilkul free hai — koi registration fee ya hidden charge nahi hai.

Q: Support se contact kaise karein?

Ans: Neeche diye gaye Contact section se seedha WhatsApp par apna sawaal likh kar bhej sakte ho, ya floating WhatsApp button use kar sakte ho.
